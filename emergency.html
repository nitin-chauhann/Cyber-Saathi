If you suspect online fraud, use verified official channels. Do not trust phone numbers or links sent by an unknown caller.

⚠️ What to Do After an Online Scam

  1. Stop: Stop sending money and stop communicating with the suspected scammer.
  2. Contact your bank: Report unauthorised transactions using official contact details.
  3. Report quickly: For financial cyber fraud in India, call 1930 and follow the reporting instructions.
  4. Save evidence: Keep transaction IDs, screenshots, messages, phone numbers and relevant dates.
  5. Secure accounts: From a trusted device, change compromised passwords and enable stronger authentication.
  6. Check your devices: If you installed a suspicious app or granted remote access, disconnect it and get help securing your device.

🔒 Important Safety Reminder

CyberSaathi is an educational project. It does not directly contact police, banks or cybercrime authorities and cannot block a transaction for you. Official websites and helpline availability may change, so verify details using trusted government or service-provider sources.

Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, banking password or remote-access code with someone claiming to help you.
