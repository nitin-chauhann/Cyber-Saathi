Know the Scam Before It Tricks You

Payment & UPI

UPI Payment Scam

Fraudsters may trick people into approving a payment or entering their UPI PIN.

🚩 Warning Signs

  • A stranger asks you to scan a QR code to receive money.
  • Someone pressures you to approve a collect request.
  • You are asked to enter your UPI PIN to receive money.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Read every payment request carefully. Never share your UPI PIN or approve requests you do not recognise.

🔐
Identity & OTP

OTP and KYC Fraud

A caller or message may pretend to be from a bank or service provider to steal your credentials.

🚩 Warning Signs

  • Someone asks you to disclose an OTP or PIN.
  • You are threatened with immediate account blocking.
  • An unexpected link asks for sensitive details.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords. Verify account issues using the official app or number.

🎣
Online & Shopping

Phishing Links

Fake websites and messages imitate trusted organisations to collect passwords or payment details.

🚩 Warning Signs

  • A suspicious or misspelled domain name.
  • Urgent threats or unexpected prize claims.
  • A link asks you to sign in or pay unexpectedly.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Open the official website yourself instead of following unexpected links. HTTPS alone does not prove a site is genuine.

💼
Job & Investment

Fake Job Offers

Scammers advertise attractive jobs and then demand fees or sensitive personal information.

🚩 Warning Signs

  • A job promises unusually high earnings for little work.
  • You must pay a fee to unlock a job or salary.
  • The recruiter uses only unofficial contact details.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Verify the employer through its official website and trusted contacts. Do not pay suspicious recruitment fees.

🛍️
Online & Shopping

Fake Shopping Deals

Fake stores or sellers advertise tempting discounts and may take payment without delivering goods.

🚩 Warning Signs

  • Prices seem unrealistically low.
  • There are no verifiable business details.
  • The seller insists on an unusual payment method.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Check independent reviews, seller details, return policies and secure payment options before buying.

📈
Job & Investment

Investment Scams

Fraudulent schemes use fake returns or social pressure to persuade people to transfer money.

🚩 Warning Signs

  • Guaranteed profits with no meaningful risk.
  • Pressure to invest immediately.
  • Requests to transfer funds to unknown accounts.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Research independently, check the relevant regulator and never invest based only on screenshots or testimonials.

📱
Identity & OTP

SIM Swap Fraud

A criminal may try to take control of your phone number to intercept calls or messages.

🚩 Warning Signs

  • Your phone unexpectedly loses network service.
  • You receive unrequested SIM replacement alerts.
  • You see account recovery messages you did not request.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Contact your mobile operator through official channels if service suddenly stops, and secure important accounts.

🆘
Payment & UPI

Fake Customer Support

Fraudsters impersonate customer service agents and try to gain access to devices or accounts.

🚩 Warning Signs

  • An unsolicited caller asks for remote access.
  • You are asked for your PIN, password or OTP.
  • The caller insists you must act immediately.

🛡️ Stay Safe

Find customer support details through the official app or website. Never install remote-access software at a stranger's request.

⚠️ If You Think You Have Been Scammed

  1. Stop communicating with the suspected scammer.
  2. Contact your bank or payment provider using official contact details if money or payment details are involved.
  3. Save screenshots, messages, transaction IDs and other evidence.
  4. Change compromised passwords from a trusted device and secure affected accounts.
  5. Report the incident through the appropriate official channel.

🇮🇳 Report Cyber Fraud in India

If you have experienced financial cyber fraud in India, call the national cyber fraud helpline 1930 as soon as possible and submit a report through the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
