🛡️ Cyber Safety Tips

Small steps, stronger security. Stay safe in the digital world!

🔐 1. Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords for different accounts. A password manager can help you store them securely.

📱 2. Never Share OTPs

Never share your OTP, PIN or verification code with anyone, even if they claim to be from your bank.

🔗 3. Check Unknown Links

Verify the website address before clicking links. Be careful with unexpected messages, prizes and urgent offers.

🛡️ 4. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Turn on two-factor authentication wherever available to add another layer of account protection.

📶 5. Use Secure Networks

Avoid sensitive transactions on untrusted public Wi-Fi. Use a trusted network and keep your device secure.

🔄 6. Update Your Devices

Install software and security updates regularly to fix vulnerabilities and protect your devices.

🎣 7. Identify Phishing

Look for suspicious sender addresses, unexpected attachments and messages pressuring you to act immediately.

💳 8. Verify Before Paying

Check payment requests carefully. Never enter your UPI PIN to receive money or approve an unknown collect request.

🔒 9. Protect Personal Data

Think before sharing your address, phone number, identity documents or live location online.

Stay Alert. Stay Secure. Stay CyberSaathi! 🛡️
